Ray Martin stats: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Ray Martin stats is a private tool used only by Ray Martin with his own TikTok account.

What data it reads. With Ray's permission (TikTok sign-in), the tool reads:

  • his basic profile: display name and avatar
  • his account statistics: follower, following, likes and video counts
  • his public videos: views, likes, comments, shares, date, title and link

How it is used. The tool uses this data only to show Ray his own numbers, and to keep a private copy of them in his own notes so he can plan content and compare posts.

Where it is kept. The data and the sign-in token are kept on Ray's own computer. They are not uploaded to any other service, and they are never sold, shared or used for advertising.

Other people's data. The tool does not collect data about any other TikTok user.

Deleting data. Ray can disconnect the app at any time in TikTok (Settings and privacy, then Security and permissions, then Apps and services). He can also delete the local copy. To ask about this policy, write to claudinecodela@gmail.com.

Changes. This policy may change. The date at the top shows the latest version.
