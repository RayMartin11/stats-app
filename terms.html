Ray Martin stats: Terms of Service

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Ray Martin stats is a private tool made by Ray Martin, an independent musician in Singapore, for his own use.

  1. Who can use it. Only Ray Martin uses this tool, with his own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public, and nobody else can sign in.
  2. What it does. After Ray signs in with TikTok, the tool reads his own profile numbers (followers, likes, video count) and his own public videos' view, like, comment and share counts. It shows them to him so he can plan his music content.
  3. What it does not do. It never posts, comments, likes, follows, messages or changes anything on TikTok, and it reads no other person's private data.
  4. TikTok's terms. Use of TikTok data through this tool also follows the TikTok Terms of Service and the TikTok Developer Terms.
  5. No warranty. The tool is provided as is, without any guarantee.
